## How It Works

### 1. Capture sources
Paste article URLs. Upload PDFs. Copy text from anywhere. Each source joins your knowledge base.

### 2. Automatic indexing
Dossier Lab extracts text, chunks content, generates embeddings, and builds a hybrid search index. You do nothing.

### 3. Search your intelligence
Find sources by meaning, not just keywords. "What have I learned about RAG systems?" returns relevant content even if you never used those exact words.

### 4. Ask and synthesize
"What patterns exist across these articles on AI adoption?"  
"Compare the three perspectives I've saved on evaluation methods."  
"What evidence do I have about cost-performance tradeoffs?"

Every answer cites specific sources you can verify.

### 5. Generate intelligence outputs
Turn collections of sources into research briefs, comparative analyses, and decision memos—in minutes instead of hours.
